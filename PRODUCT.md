# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Japanese high school students studying at home, often late at night the day before a 定期テスト or in the weeks before 共通テスト. Mostly on a phone at a desk with a textbook open. They are stuck on a specific problem, may feel embarrassed to ask a teacher or friend, and want a quick answer or hint. A second group is students who are good at a subject and like explaining it.

## Product Purpose
「テスト前ヘルプ板」 is an anonymous Q&A board for students to help each other. They post a question tagged by subject, optionally with a photo of the problem, and mark it 「わかった！」 when it is solved. It succeeds when asking feels low-stakes, unsolved questions are easy to spot by subject, and posting takes under a minute on a phone.

## Brand Personality
Like a friend's well-organized study notebook: warm, encouraging, tidy. The voice is a friendly classmate (casual です/ます mixed with plain form), never a teacher or a corporation. Emotional goals: "it's okay to ask", calm focus, a small win when something gets solved.

## Anti-references
- Generic corporate SaaS: blue primary buttons on white cards, stock startup look
- Childish or elementary-school: cartoon mascots, rainbow palettes, rounded-everything toy look
- Old 2ch-style text walls (dense, gray, tiny type)

## Design Principles
1. **Asking is safe.** Anonymous by default; inviting, judgment-free copy and visuals.
2. **The question is the hero.** The problem text and photo come first; chrome stays quiet.
3. **Solved should feel good.** 「わかった！」 is the emotional peak of the product; reward it.
4. **Phone at a desk first.** Thumb-reachable, readable at arm's length, fast on school Wi-Fi.
5. **Notebook, not toy.** Study-material touches (rules, highlighter, tabs) used with restraint, for a teen audience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Subjects are never told apart by color alone (always a text label). Japanese typography: comfortable line-height (≥1.7 for body) and font sizes ≥16px on mobile.
